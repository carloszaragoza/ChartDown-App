# ChartDown App – Design Handoff

**Version:** 2.0 · **Date:** October 6, 2026 · **Status:** design approved

Version 2 moves the app to the **Astryx** design system. It replaces version 1, "Papel y tinta" (October 3, 2026), whose files stay in `design/` as history. This sheet turns the approved design into exact rules for building the app; anything not covered here is in `design/astryx/`:

- `design/astryx/screens/light/` and `screens/dark/` – a screenshot of every screen and tab, 1280 px wide, in both modes.
- `design/astryx/SCREENS.md` – one section per screen: purpose, specs, Astryx components, states and interactions.
- `design/astryx/prototype.html` – the clickable prototype; `reference-code/` – its source and pinned dependencies.
- `design/logo/` – the logo kit (section 7).

The redesign is built in the blocks of E13 (`lista-tareas.md`); until a screen's block is merged, that screen keeps its version 1 look.

## 1. Principles the code must respect

1. **Color means something, one meaning per color** (owner's decision, October 27, 2026, replacing "red = music director and instruments"): **Rosewood**, ChartDown's red, = the music director (the theme makes Astryx's red Rosewood: `#94484A` text on `#F6E8E7` in light, `#E5ABA8` on `#33201F` in dark, so `color="red"` tokens are Rosewood; the error color stays Astryx's own red); **purple** (`color="purple"`) = instruments; **blue** = indications and dynamics, links and selection; **yellow** (the warning color) = a problem to fix in a chart, in the editors only. The **brand red**, signal red `#D12F26` on light and `#FF7A6B` on dark (token `--brand`; owner's decision, October 28, 2026: Rosewood looked dull as a brand), is the logo's first slash and the landing page's accents (the headline's last line, its tags, the marquee's slashes, the feature numbers); it carries no meaning in a chart, where Rosewood stays the MD's alone. Never decoration, and never color alone: each comes with its word and its icon (the MD's baton, the instrument's icon, the indication's flag, the problem's bar with an exclamation mark). Every pair passes WCAG AA.
2. **Never color alone.** Every music director's note says "MD" or the instrument's name; every status dot has a visible text label beside it.
3. **The numbers win.** In a chart only chord numbers and bar lines are drawn in the primary text color; everything else is secondary.
4. **Two modes, one layout.** Light and dark follow the system; they change colors, never sizes or positions.
5. **Touch targets:** at least 44 × 44 px on iPad, to tap with confidence. Astryx's default sizes are smaller (side navigation items 32 px, the language control 20 px, the collapse button 28 px, measured October 7, 2026). The app's Astryx theme (`apps/web/theme/chartdown.theme.ts`, built from neutral) raises the element sizes on touch screens (`pointer: coarse`): small 32, medium 44, large 47 px (owner's review, October 21, 2026: a tenth smaller on an iPad, medium kept at the 44 px floor); the side navigation uses the large size. Desktop with a mouse keeps Astryx's sizes. A control is never shrunk below 44 px on touch. **Stand mode is stricter**, since it is played live: on touch, each song-form section and Previous / Next are at least 50 × 50 px (56 until the owner's review of October 21, 2026) with at least 8 px between sections; the abbreviation is large and bold, the current section is filled (never only underlined), and a repeated section shows which time it is (B1, B1², B1³; read out as "Bridge 1, 2nd time"). Its other controls (Letters, Stage, Full screen) keep 44 px with a visible outline (owner's review, October 7, 2026). On the pages that show a whole chart (Version, Song in a set, the offline reader, and the chart editor's preview) the song form **stays pinned at the top** while the chart scrolls, so any section is one tap away (owner's review, October 7, 2026); there it is read, not played, so it and the metronome take the medium size, 44 px on touch as the side navigation's items, the section names a size above the labels and bold, each section at least square, and the actions and View options the same 44 px with the side navigation's icon size (owner's review, October 22, 2026, T-2833); a phone keeps stand mode's 40 px for the song form and the metronome.
   **One size for every button, stand mode included (owner's review, October 30, 2026, T-3036).** A button, a menu, a field and a tab are the ChartDown theme's medium size wherever they are, so stand mode's buttons read as the app's when it is navigated: 44 px on an iPad, from the theme's touch sizes; the side navigation keeps its own rows (Astryx `SideNav`), and the audio player's Play is the same on every page. Icons take their size from what holds them, as Astryx says (a medium button holds a 16 px icon), never one of their own: ChartDown's own icons (the metronome, Live) go through Astryx's `Icon` too. The chart's numbers keep their size, since they are the chart; the song form's section buttons are buttons, at the app's size too (owner's review, October 30, 2026).
6. **One gutter.** Every page in the app shell keeps `--spacing-8` (32 px at neutral's density, 28.8 px at ChartDown's, principle 10) between its content and the side navigation, the top and the other edges: Astryx pages with `Layout padding={8}`, version 1 pages with the same padding until they are redesigned. Fields and buttons never run past that edge. Stand mode and the landing page set their own (owner's review, October 7, 2026).
7. **A tap answers at once.** On the pages with the side navigation, a tap on a link to another page shows skeletons of a page in its place until it arrives (Astryx `Skeleton`, with a status for screen readers), never the old page frozen; the client draws them, so the server's answer (a 404 included) is unchanged. A tap that stays on the same page shows none. Links prefetch on intent (pointer over, touch, keyboard focus), not on sight (F-003, October 8, 2026).
8. **Icons for row actions.** A list row's actions (open, edit, director's notes, remove…) are icon buttons with their name as a tooltip and an accessible name, never words, to save space; a page's main action keeps its words. Every row of a list is a tap target as a whole when it leads somewhere (owner's review, October 16, 2026).
9. **One kind of list row (owner's review on iPad, October 29, 2026, T-3035).** A row that leads somewhere is a tap target whole, as the catalog's songs are: a tap anywhere on it opens what it names, and it shows so, with the row's hover and press and a pointer, never only its title as a link or a button at its end. Its own buttons (a band's Sets, a step's action, a report's Dismiss and Hide) still answer for themselves, and the row adds no second tab stop (Astryx `ListItem` with `href`, or its `interactiveRef` on the row's link). A button that opens the same place as its row is not repeated (a report's View goes). A row that only says something (a lineup, a band's members, a sign-in method, a note) is not a tap target and doesn't look like one. Every list has dividers and the same edge alignment (`hasDividers`, `edge="outset"`), its rows `ListItem`, never a bare `Item`, which ignores both: a row's text starts and ends on the lines of the page's title and content, and its divider and hover reach a little past them (owner's review, November 1, 2026, T-3045). A table sits in a `Card` with its title, so its lines reach the card's edges, never into the page's gutter.
10. **Density (owner's review on an iPad, October 30, 2026, T-3042).** Everything is a tenth smaller than Astryx's neutral, the type and the spacing, on every screen (laptops, iPads, Surface, phones), and on a touch screen under 1024 px too, where neutral sets larger type: the ChartDown theme's tokens are neutral's times 0.9. The controls are not: their heights stay the theme's (`--size-element-*`), 44 px on a touch screen at least, the iPad standard, and 32 px on a phone held upright (R-03.9). A list's row is a touch target too: on a touch screen it is at least as tall as a control (44 px), whatever the density does to its text.

## 2. Design system: Astryx

- **Library:** `@astryxdesign/core` with the `@astryxdesign/theme-neutral` theme (version 0.6.5, MIT), icons from `@heroicons/react`. `@stylexjs/stylex` is its peer; `@astryxdesign/cli` is a development tool.
- **Theme:** the root layout wraps the app in `<Theme theme={neutralTheme} mode="system">`, after `reset.css`, `astryx.css` and the theme's `theme.css`.
- **Use Astryx components for everything they cover:** `AppShell`, `SideNav`, `Layout` (`LayoutHeader`, `LayoutContent`, `LayoutPanel`), `List`/`Item`, `Table`, `TabList`/`Tab`, `Card`, `Section`, `Button`, `TextInput`, `TextArea`, `Selector`, `RadioList`, `CheckboxList`, `Switch`, `SegmentedControl`, `DateInput`, `TimeInput`, `Token`, `Badge`, `StatusDot`, `Avatar`, `Banner`, `EmptyState`, `Breadcrumbs`, `Heading`, `Text`, `Icon`, and the layout stacks (`VStack`, `HStack`, `Grid`). Dense data goes in rows (`Table`, `List`), never in a card per item; a status is a `StatusDot` or `Token`; a `Badge` is only for counts.
- **Check a component's API before using it** with the Astryx CLI (`npx astryx component <Name> --dense`); never guess props.
- **No raw colors, pixel sizes or custom CSS.** Use component props and theme tokens (`var(--color-*)`, `var(--spacing-*)`, `var(--radius-*)`, `var(--border-width)`). The only custom visuals are the chart components and the logo (section 3).
- **Fonts:** the theme's Figtree for the interface and its monospace stack for code. Figtree is served by the app (SIL Open Font License), never from the internet, so a downloaded set opens with no connection.

## 5. Responsive

- **iPad landscape (1180 px) and desktop:** the reference layout, with the side navigation open.
- **iPad portrait and narrower screens:** side panels move below the content wherever the content would keep less than 480 px beside them, the side navigation open or not (owner's review, October 30, 2026: the set's lineup crowded its songs on an iPad upright); rows of four bars stay.
- **Stand mode's top bar on an iPad (owner's review on a regular iPad, October 29, 2026, T-3032):** actions apart from settings, and every button with its icon and its word, never an icon alone and never a word cut short; only the way back stays a quiet icon button with its name in a tooltip. The **actions**, what is done while playing: the set's songs, the metronome (Play with the tempo on it, counting while it plays), **Listen** (the band's audio) and **Live** for the MD. The **settings**, what is set once, behind one **Settings** button at the end (a gear, its word beside it), opening a bottom sheet in **three tabs**, by what the musician does (owner's request, October 29, 2026, T-3034): **Chart**, what is read (View as for the MD, Numbers / Letters, Beat marks, Show notes); **Screen**, how it looks (Theme: Light or Stage, Number size, Coming up, Full screen; Motion under **More**); **Metronome**, how it sounds (the tempo's − and + with **Tap tempo** on the same row, Click, Count-in; under **More** the levels of the beat, eighths and sixteenths, Practice speed, the beat line and Keep speakers awake). What is changed during a rehearsal shows; what is set once waits under More, closed. Each tab fits one iPad screen; Settings opens on Chart, then on the tab last used while stand mode stays open. On an iPad the sheet's heading is Settings, with no repeat of the song's name, already on the bar; a phone's sheet starts with the song, its bar having no room for it. The actions are ordered by how much they are used (owner's review, October 30, 2026, T-3036): the **metronome** first, which everyone uses; **Listen**; **Live**, for the MD only; then the **set's songs**; **Settings** last. The bar is always one line, the way back and the song first, the actions after them (owner's review, October 30, 2026): when room runs short, the song's title shortens with an ellipsis ("King Of My Hea…"), never the controls, which never go to a second line. "Starts at bar 3", once a bar is tapped (R-11.2), is said first in the song's line under the title ("Starts at bar 3 · Key G · 4/4 · …"), never beside the controls. **Live** has its own ChartDown icon (Astryx's 24 px grid, 1.5 px line): a dot sending waves to both sides. A phone keeps its one-line bar, its Settings an icon button. **Cards** are compact (same review): less room inside each card and each bar (the theme's spacing 2 around a bar's chords), less room between cards, the section's name a step smaller; the numbers keep their size, and Number size still changes them. The band's audio shows its icon only on this bar, at the app's size and filled as the metronome is, its name in a tooltip and for screen readers (owner's review, October 30, 2026). The song's title never disappears: when the actions leave it less than a readable width, Settings and the set's songs show their icons only, their names in a tooltip and for screen readers, and "Song 3 of 8" goes beside the set's name above the title; the metronome, the audio and Live keep their place. The song form is one row on every screen, scrolling sideways with the current section kept in view, never a second row (owner's review, October 30, 2026).
- **Phone (R-03.8, owner's request, October 14, 2026):** narrower than 600 CSS px, or shorter than 500 px in landscape. No screen may break or scroll sideways, and the side navigation becomes a drawer. Every screen is designed for it (R-03.9, owner's request, October 15, 2026):
  - **Stand mode (version and set song):** one top bar on one line: Close (or Set) as an icon button, the song's title with "Section 1 of 14" (in a set, "Song 2 of 5 · Section 1 of 14"), a **View options** icon button, and Previous and Next as icon buttons (the app's one size, as every button), Next filled. ## 6. Accessibility

- AA contrast in both modes (the Astryx theme's).
- Real buttons, links and inputs; never clickable elements without a role.
- Every icon-only control has an `aria-label`; the song form announces its state and notes.
- Visible keyboard focus everywhere; tab order top to bottom, start to end.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## 7. Logo and icons

The logo is **concept 3C, "One bar"**: one bar of a chart, two bar lines and four rhythm slashes; the first slash, in red pencil, is the music director's cue that starts the band. The kit lives in `design/logo/` (its guide: `design/logo/guide.pdf`).

- **Versions:** horizontal (symbol + name, the default), stacked (symbol above the name, for square and narrow spaces), symbol alone (app icon, avatar, favicon), and the two-beat **small symbol** for 24 px and below. "App" versions add "App", always secondary to the name.
- **Colors:** ink `#16212C` on light, `#E6EDF3` on dark, for the bar lines, the slashes and the name; the brand red, signal red `#D12F26` on light and `#FF7A6B` on dark (owner's decision, October 28, 2026; it was red pencil `#B8322A` / `#FF8478`, then Rosewood for a day), **for the first slash only**. In the app the logo takes them from the tokens (the primary text color and `--brand`), so it follows light and dark. Black and white versions for one-color uses.
- **Rules:** clear space of at least half the symbol's height on every side; symbol at least 16 px, horizontal logo at least 96 px wide; never stretched, rotated, all red, shadowed or with slashes added or removed. Red is an accent, never the meaning: the logo reads the same in one color.
- **Where:** the side navigation's header and the landing page show the horizontal logo, with the accessible name "ChartDown"; the favicon (an SVG that follows the browser's light or dark theme, plus ICO), the iPad and Apple touch icons, the web app manifest (192, 512 and maskable 512 px) and the Open Graph image for link previews come from the kit.
- **ChartDown's own icons** (owner's decision, October 27, 2026: not generic ones): drawn for ChartDown on Astryx's icon grid, 24 px, 1.5 px line, round ends, in the text's color, musical where they can be (bar lines, repeat dots, the baton). They live in `@chartdown/ui` (`icons.ts` and `notationIcons.tsx`); the chart's colors take the MD's baton, the indication's flag and the problem's icons, and the visual editor's actions are drawn the same way (R-13.16).
- The text in every logo file is converted to outlines (Barlow Condensed Bold, "App" in Medium).

**The visual editor's icons** (SPEC-13, R-13.16): one list, `apps/web/lib/visual/icons.ts`, gives each action its icon, always beside its name. The chart's marks use the notation icons of `@chartdown/ui` (`notationIcons.tsx`): drawn as the chart draws each mark (‹ for a push, the fermata's arc and dot, |: and :| with their dots, the ending's bracket and number, segno, coda), on Astryx's 24 × 24 grid and 1.5 line, the dynamics as their bold italic letters; the editor's other actions use ChartDown's own icons drawn the same way (`EDITOR_ICONS`, owner's decision, October 27, 2026: never generic ones): undo, redo, song details, import, the band's audio, the video, the ChartDown text (`#`), save, done, add a section or a row, rename, add a note, repeat a section (the repeat signs), duplicate, move, edit the original, delete, a bar before or after (bar lines), a new row, delete a row, and the visual editor itself. The dropdowns' chevrons stay Astryx's.
