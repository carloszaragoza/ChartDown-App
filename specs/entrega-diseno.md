# ChartDown App – Design Handoff

**Version:** 2.0 · **Date:** October 6, 2026 · **Status:** design approved

Version 2 moves the app to the **Astryx** design system. It replaces version 1, "Papel y tinta" (October 3, 2026), whose files stay in `design/` as history. This sheet turns the approved design into exact rules for building the app; anything not covered here is in `design/astryx/`:

- `design/astryx/screens/light/` and `screens/dark/` – a screenshot of every screen and tab, 1280 px wide, in both modes.
- `design/astryx/SCREENS.md` – one section per screen: purpose, specs, Astryx components, states and interactions.
- `design/astryx/prototype.html` – the clickable prototype; `reference-code/` – its source and pinned dependencies.
- `design/logo/` – the logo kit (section 7).

The redesign is built in the blocks of E13 (`lista-tareas.md`); until a screen's block is merged, that screen keeps its version 1 look.

## 1. Principles the code must respect

1. **Color means something.** Red (Astryx `color="red"` tokens, the error color) = music director and instruments, never decoration. Accent (blue) = dynamics, links and selection.
2. **Never color alone.** Every music director's note says "MD" or the instrument's name; every status dot has a visible text label beside it.
3. **The numbers win.** In a chart only chord numbers and bar lines are drawn in the primary text color; everything else is secondary.
4. **Two modes, one layout.** Light and dark follow the system; they change colors, never sizes or positions.
5. **Touch targets:** at least 44 × 44 px on iPad, to tap with confidence. Astryx's default sizes are smaller (side navigation items 32 px, the language control 20 px, the collapse button 28 px, measured October 7, 2026). The app's Astryx theme (`apps/web/theme/chartdown.theme.ts`, built from neutral) raises the element sizes on touch screens (`pointer: coarse`): small 36, medium 44, large 52 px; the side navigation uses the large size. Desktop with a mouse keeps Astryx's sizes. A control is never shrunk below 44 px on touch. **Stand mode is stricter**, since it is played live: on touch, each song-form section and Previous / Next are at least 56 × 56 px with at least 8 px between sections; the abbreviation is large and bold, the current section is filled (never only underlined), and a repeated section shows which time it is (B1, B1², B1³; read out as "Bridge 1, 2nd time"). Its other controls (Letters, Stage, Full screen) keep 44 px with a visible outline (owner's review, October 7, 2026). On the pages that show a whole chart (Version, Song in a set, the offline reader) the song form uses the same 56 px buttons and **stays pinned at the top** while the chart scrolls, so any section is one tap away (owner's review, October 7, 2026).
6. **One gutter.** Every page in the app shell keeps 32 px (`--spacing-8`) between its content and the side navigation, the top and the other edges: Astryx pages with `Layout padding={8}`, version 1 pages with the same padding until they are redesigned. Fields and buttons never run past that edge. Stand mode and the landing page set their own (owner's review, October 7, 2026).
7. **A tap answers at once.** On the pages with the side navigation, a tap on a link to another page shows skeletons of a page in its place until it arrives (Astryx `Skeleton`, with a status for screen readers), never the old page frozen; the client draws them, so the server's answer (a 404 included) is unchanged. A tap that stays on the same page shows none. Links prefetch on intent (pointer over, touch, keyboard focus), not on sight (F-003, October 8, 2026).
8. **Icons for row actions.** A list row's actions (open, edit, director's notes, remove…) are icon buttons with their name as a tooltip and an accessible name, never words, to save space; a page's main action keeps its words. Every row of a list is a tap target as a whole when it leads somewhere (owner's review, October 16, 2026).

## 2. Design system: Astryx

- **Library:** `@astryxdesign/core` with the `@astryxdesign/theme-neutral` theme (version 0.6.5, MIT), icons from `@heroicons/react`. `@stylexjs/stylex` is its peer; `@astryxdesign/cli` is a development tool.
- **Theme:** the root layout wraps the app in `<Theme theme={neutralTheme} mode="system">`, after `reset.css`, `astryx.css` and the theme's `theme.css`.
- **Use Astryx components for everything they cover:** `AppShell`, `SideNav`, `Layout` (`LayoutHeader`, `LayoutContent`, `LayoutPanel`), `List`/`Item`, `Table`, `TabList`/`Tab`, `Card`, `Section`, `Button`, `TextInput`, `TextArea`, `Selector`, `RadioList`, `CheckboxList`, `Switch`, `SegmentedControl`, `DateInput`, `TimeInput`, `Token`, `Badge`, `StatusDot`, `Avatar`, `Banner`, `EmptyState`, `Breadcrumbs`, `Heading`, `Text`, `Icon`, and the layout stacks (`VStack`, `HStack`, `Grid`). Dense data goes in rows (`Table`, `List`), never in a card per item; a status is a `StatusDot` or `Token`; a `Badge` is only for counts.
- **Check a component's API before using it** with the Astryx CLI (`npx astryx component <Name> --dense`); never guess props.
- **No raw colors, pixel sizes or custom CSS.** Use component props and theme tokens (`var(--color-*)`, `var(--spacing-*)`, `var(--radius-*)`, `var(--border-width)`). The only custom visuals are the chart components and the logo (section 3).
- **Fonts:** the theme's Figtree for the interface and its monospace stack for code. Figtree is served by the app (SIL Open Font License), never from the internet, so a downloaded set opens with no connection.

## 3. Custom components (`packages/ui`)

| Component | Rules |
|---|---|
| **BarRow** | A row of bars of a chart, rendered from the `chartdown` tree (never a simplified parser), drawn by the chart viewer rules in `design/chart-viewer/CHART-VIEWER.md` (approved October 7, 2026), which replace the reference code's chart rendering. Each bar has a tags line (ending label, dynamic, cues, ×N on the last bar of a repeat), a chord line on a grid of 12 columns per beat (each chord starts at its beat and spans its length; a dashed line marks a change inside the bar; no length text), and a beat line with one slash per beat in three strengths by meter; a lyric landmark under it. Every bar of a row shares those lines, so the chord numbers of a row stand on one baseline. Marks: push `‹`, stab `^`, fermata `𝄐`, hold as the chord centered in a diamond, N.C., `%` and the tie. Repeats draw double bar lines; endings a label and a top line; a key change a "Key change: A" line above its row. Chord numbers in Barlow Condensed at `4xl` in stand mode (`lg`), `3xl` in the viewer and editors (`md`) and `2xl` in the next-section preview (`sm`, in the secondary color). Its tokens (`chart-tokens.css`) map to Astryx theme tokens and are declared on the chart itself, so each theme scope (stand mode's too) resolves them; dynamics and marks use the theme's blue text token (`--color-text-blue`), since the neutral theme's accent is the ink. A row has as many columns as bars. Inside a bar, the chords keep `--spacing-4` from the bar lines and `--spacing-2` from a split's dashed line, more than the prototype, so a number never sits against a line (owner, October 7, 2026). Rows never shrink a bar below its minimum width: a row that doesn't fit scrolls sideways inside itself. |
| **MDNotes** | A music director's notes: a red `Token` with "MD" (for everyone) or the instrument's name and icon, followed by the note's text. |
| **Instrument icons** | Drums, bass, keys, guitar, vocals, saxophone, trumpet, trombone, violin: stroke icons on a 24 px grid, the same drawings as version 1. Red only inside instrument contexts. An instrument without an icon shows its name only. |

## 5. Responsive

- **iPad landscape (1180 px) and desktop:** the reference layout, with the side navigation open.
- **iPad portrait and narrower screens:** side panels move below the content; rows of four bars stay.
- **Phone (R-03.8, owner's request, October 14, 2026):** narrower than 600 CSS px, or shorter than 500 px in landscape. No screen may break or scroll sideways, and the side navigation becomes a drawer. Every screen is designed for it (R-03.9, owner's request, October 15, 2026):
  - **Stand mode (version and set song):** one top bar on one line: Close (or Set) as an icon button, the song's title with "Section 1 of 14" (in a set, "Song 2 of 5 · Section 1 of 14"), a **View options** icon button, and Previous and Next as icon buttons (48 px on a phone), Next filled. View options opens a bottom sheet with the song's title, key, meter and tempo, Numbers / Letters, Beats, Stage, Full screen and, for the MD, View as. The song form is one row of its 56 px buttons that scrolls sideways inside itself, with the current section kept in view; held sideways it takes the title's place in the top bar, so the bars get the height. The server draws the phone layout from the browser's user agent, so a phone never sees the iPad layout first. The next-section preview is its line alone ("Next: Verse · 8 bars"), with no bars. Swiping the chart left or right moves to the next or previous section, as on the iPad (section 4, 03 Stand mode).
  - **Bar rows:** in portrait, a row wider than the screen wraps into lines of two bars (a row of four reads as 2 + 2) and the chords are drawn at the viewer's size (`md`); in landscape rows of four stay. A bar never shrinks below its minimum width; a single bar wider than the screen still scrolls inside its row.
  - **Version page:** the header's actions wrap under the title, the display choices take two lines at most (Numbers / Letters and the key, then Beats), the pinned song form is the same scrolling strip, and the first section's first row is on the first screen.
  - **Pages with a side panel** (Home, a set, Edit set, Bands, a band's sets, the chart editor, the director's notes, Administration's instruments): one column, the main content first and the panel after it, full width; the notes editor's section list comes first, above the section it edits.
  - **Choices:** a SegmentedControl keeps at most three short options on a phone; View as, the catalog's source and any longer choice become a `Selector` with the same options and label.
  - **Lists with actions** (Your versions, a set's songs, a band's members): the title and its details on their own lines, the actions under them, wrapping; never a title squeezed out by its buttons.
  - **Page headers:** the actions wrap under the title at their full label, never "…".
  - **A set's song:** the song form strip scrolls inside itself on its own line, and the metronome's sound and Play buttons sit on the line under it.
  - **The chart editor:** the text area takes the full width and wraps nothing; the preview follows below it.
  - **Control sizes:** on a phone held upright (narrower than 640 px, touch) controls are a little smaller than on an iPad so the screen holds more than buttons (owner's review, October 15, 2026): 32, 40 and 44 px for the small, medium and large sizes, and 48 px for stand mode's section and turn buttons; an iPad keeps 36, 44 and 52 px, and 56 px in stand mode. The 32 px gutter becomes 16 px (`--spacing-4`) on a phone.

## 6. Accessibility

- AA contrast in both modes (the Astryx theme's).
- Real buttons, links and inputs; never clickable elements without a role.
- Every icon-only control has an `aria-label`; the song form announces its state and notes.
- Visible keyboard focus everywhere; tab order top to bottom, start to end.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## 7. Logo and icons

The logo is **concept 3C, "One bar"**: one bar of a chart, two bar lines and four rhythm slashes; the first slash, in red pencil, is the music director's cue that starts the band. The kit lives in `design/logo/` (its guide: `design/logo/guide.pdf`).

- **Versions:** horizontal (symbol + name, the default), stacked (symbol above the name, for square and narrow spaces), symbol alone (app icon, avatar, favicon), and the two-beat **small symbol** for 24 px and below. "App" versions add "App", always secondary to the name.
- **Colors:** ink `#16212C` on light, `#E6EDF3` on dark, for the bar lines, the slashes and the name; red pencil `#B8322A` on light, `#FF8478` on dark, **for the first slash only**. In the app the logo takes them from the theme (the primary text color and the red), so it follows light and dark. Black and white versions for one-color uses.
- **Rules:** clear space of at least half the symbol's height on every side; symbol at least 16 px, horizontal logo at least 96 px wide; never stretched, rotated, all red, shadowed or with slashes added or removed. Red is an accent, never the meaning: the logo reads the same in one color.
- **Where:** the side navigation's header and the landing page show the horizontal logo, with the accessible name "ChartDown"; the favicon (an SVG that follows the browser's light or dark theme, plus ICO), the iPad and Apple touch icons, the web app manifest (192, 512 and maskable 512 px) and the Open Graph image for link previews come from the kit.
- The text in every logo file is converted to outlines (Barlow Condensed Bold, "App" in Medium).
