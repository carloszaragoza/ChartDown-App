ChartDown logo · usage guide

One bar of a chart: two bar lines and four rhythm slashes. The first beat, in the brand red, is the cue that starts the band.

Horizontal

Default. Use it wherever there is room.

Stacked

For square and narrow spaces.

Symbol

App icon, avatar, favicon.

Clear space

x

Keep at least x = half the symbol's height empty on every side. Nothing else enters that area.

Minimum size

Symbol 16 px minimum; horizontal logo 96 px wide minimum. At 24 px and below use the two-beat small symbol (files named symbol-small).

Colors

On lightOn dark
Ink: bar lines, slashes, name#16212C#E6EDF3
Brand red (signal red): first slash only#D12F26#FF7A6B
Backgrounds#FBFCFD#17212B

Red is an accent, never the meaning: the logo reads the same in one color, so it works for color-blind readers. The brand red is the logo's and the landing page's; in the app's charts the music director keeps Rosewood (#94484A).

Backgrounds and one color

Full color on light and dark stage mode; black or white for single-color print, embroidery and engraving.

Don't

Don't stretch or squash it.

Don't rotate or tilt it.

Don't make the whole mark red.

Don't add shadows, gradients or textures.

Don't add or remove slashes.

Don't place it on busy or low-contrast backgrounds.

Typography

  • Name: Barlow Condensed Bold, converted to outlines in every file. "App" is set in Barlow Condensed Medium, smaller, and is always secondary to ChartDown.
  • Interface and body text: Figtree (the app's Astryx theme). Both fonts are under the SIL Open Font License.
